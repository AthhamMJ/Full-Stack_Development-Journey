Claim 01

The rule is four words

Turn, then grow. No plant measures an angle, remembers the last leaf, or counts anything. The pattern falls out of chemistry that has no idea it is making a pattern.

Claim 02

It runs at every scale

Pine cone: 8 spirals one way, 13 the other. Pineapple: 8, 13, 21. Sunflower: 34, 55, sometimes 89. Same list, different organism, no contact between them.

Claim 03

Nothing is coordinating it

There is no shared clock and no plan. There is a rule short enough to write on a thumbnail, executing separately, everywhere, at once.

If reality runs on rules this short, the question isn't whether something is running it.

It's how many layers deep this goes.
